Wisdom Beyond Machine

Along the path of knowledge, I walk each day, Carrying dreams of teaching along the way; AI is a tool the curious mind can use, To deepen lessons and help learners choose.

Inside the classroom, lessons are shaped, Technology guides us, keeps learning safe; It does not steal the teacher’s role or pride, But stands as a partner by the teacher’s side.

In research and study, data grows strong, Learning becomes honest, clear, and well-drawn; Yet behind the speed machines can provide, Critical thinking must still be our guide.

As a future teacher, I carry a task, To use AI wisely and questions to ask; Quick answers alone are never enough, If lessons lack meaning and thinking is rough.

Values and ethics, awareness and care, No digital program can fully compare; In the bond of teacher and learner so true, Lessons grow human, moral, and true.

So as we move on in modern education’s way, AI will be used with respect every day; So the youth we teach, guide, and inspire, May be critical, responsible, and human always.

“Wisdom Beyond Machines”

On the path of learning, I take my way, With dreams to guide students every day; AI helps us think and find what’s true, It makes learning faster, helping us through.

In the classroom, lessons grow and shine, Technology helps, but the teacher’s fine; It does not steal the teacher’s pride, It stands beside them, a helpful guide.

In research, data grows and flows, Studying becomes easy as it goes; But behind the speed that machines provide, Critical thinking must always guide.

Teachers of the future have a role to play, AI must be used in a careful way; Quick answers alone are not enough, Understanding the lesson is the right stuff.

Values, ethics, and knowing what’s right, No program can teach with heart so bright; In the bond of teacher and student strong, Learning shapes humans to last lifelong.

So in modern learning, we must be wise, Use AI with care, and open our eyes; To help the youth think, care, and grow, Critical, responsible, with hearts that glow.
